# The Last Screen Standing — the full story, in order

One continuous night, told in the order the game actually plays it. Every
beat below names the clip that plays under it — marked **[HAVE]** for the
nine you've already got, or **[TO GENERATE]** for the eight in
[`CLIP-GENERATION-SCRIPT.md`](CLIP-GENERATION-SCRIPT.md) — so you can check
each one off as you match it up. Nothing here is invented for this
document; every line of narrative is the actual text the game shows,
pulled straight from [`questions.ts`](../src/survival/questions.ts).

---

## 0. Lobby

**Clip: `lobby.mp4` — [TO GENERATE, §1]**

Before a word of story plays, the room is just waiting — a room code on
screen, people trickling in. Nothing has happened yet. This is the calm
the rest of the night breaks.

## 1. Briefing

**Clip: `briefing.mp4` — [TO GENERATE, §2]**

This is the world, stated once, in full, before anyone touches a choice:

> The world ends on a Tuesday. A bio-digital virus — part organic disease,
> part corrupted smart-tech — is going through the city. The military are
> shutting down the grid and calling it a quarantine. You are at your desk
> at home. The last extraction chopper leaves the TWIN international trade
> port, on the far edge of the city, and you intend to be on it.

And immediately after, the one mechanic everyone will need for the next
nine rounds, stated in full so nobody can claim they weren't told:

> TWIN publishes its berth manifest to the IOTA ledger, and the ledger
> doesn't care that the grid is down — every screen you pass tonight will
> carry one line of it. A line reads berth N, seal D, plus a verdict:
> signed green (it counts) or red, rejected (ignore it). Berth is a
> position, 1 to 4. Once you have all four green seals, read them from
> berth 4 down to berth 1 — that is the code. Some rounds carry nothing to
> read at all. You get two tries at the keypad — a wrong one costs you a
> few seconds before the next — and if the second is wrong too, the port
> stops listening and it is the room who decides for you. Nobody is going
> to write any of this down for you.

The `briefing.mp4` clip is the establishing shot for both of these — the
skyline losing power, the port lit up on the horizon as the one place still
running. It plays under both paragraphs; there's no separate clip per
paragraph.

## 2. Warm-up 1 — First Move

**Clip: `first-move.mp4` — [TO GENERATE, §3]**

Zero stakes. The alarm, before anything real has happened yet:

> Every phone in the house screams at once. Before anything else — what's

*(Your charger / The good snacks / A family photo / Your gaming console /

**Clip: `group-chat.mp4` — [TO GENERATE, §4]**

## 5. Question 2 — The Feuding Neighbour

## 6. Question 3 — The Drive

**Clip: `the-drive.mp4` — [HAVE]**
**Manifest: none this round.**

The road itself becomes the obstacle:

> The road to the port is a crawl of brake lights. A family is banging on
> windows two cars over, begging anyone for room. You have exactly one
> open seat, and the gap in traffic will not stay open long.

*(Open the door for the closest stranger / Only take someone who can prove
they are clean / Speed up and pretend you did not see them / Point them
toward a bus you saw idling back / Let them argue it out, take the winner)*

## 7. Question 4 — The Checkpoint

**Clip: `checkpoint.mp4` — [HAVE]**
**Manifest: berth 4, GREEN — second puzzle piece.**

The port's own perimeter, and the first sign the state itself is failing:

> The outer perimeter of the TWIN port. The military are scanning for
> bio-digital signatures, the line has stopped moving, and a riot is
> building at the back of it.

*(Shove a cougher into the AI scanner / Hi-vis vest, clipboard, walk
briskly / Wait in line and keep your head down / Help the guard calm the
crowd / Crawl through the storm drain)*

## 8. Question 5 — The Infiltration

**Clip: `infiltration.mp4` — [HAVE]**
**Manifest: berth 4, red (decoy — same berth as The Checkpoint, deliberately

> You are inside the port, and the smart-gates into the secure logistics

*(A dead worker's ID on the IOTA terminal / Type admin and password into

## 9. Question 6 — The Supply Cache

**Clip: `supply-cache.mp4` — [HAVE]**
**Manifest: berth 2, GREEN — third puzzle piece.**

Someone else has already found this room:

> A warehouse breakroom. Glass shatters down the hall — they are inside

*(Water and high-calorie protein paste / The industrial CO2 fire

## 10. Question 7 — The Loading Dock

**Clip: `loading-dock.mp4` — [HAVE]**
**Manifest: berth 1, red (decoy — same berth as The Feuding Neighbour).**

The first choice about someone the room will never see:

> Halfway across the dock, a survivor radio crackles to life on the

*(Give them the real route to the stairwell / Give them a decoy route

## 11. Question 8 — The Dark Stairwell

**Clip: `dark-stairwell.mp4` — [HAVE]**
**Manifest: none this round — the darkest choice in the game, and it's the
one round with nothing to read at all.**

Thirty flights, blind:

> The grid fails. Thirty flights to the helipad in total darkness, with
> frantic movement echoing both above you and below you.

*(Trip the stranger running beside you / Throw loose change down the
stairwell / Shoes off, climb in total silence / Hold your phone light up
for the group / Climb outside the railing, over the drop)*

---

## 12. Question 9 — The Roof Breach

**Clip: `roof-breach.mp4` — [HAVE]**
**Manifest: berth 3, GREEN — the fourth and final puzzle piece. All four
green seals should be readable from the Exchange by the end of this
round.**

The last door, and the chopper is already visible:

> The roof. The hydraulic blast doors are buckling behind you, and the
> extraction chopper is already on its approach.

*(Smear yourself in infected blood / Wrap up in a silver space blanket /
Push a generator against the door / Stand at the door and hold them off /
Jam the door's hydraulic gears)*

Right after this round's reveal, the room hears the extraction rule
restated once, as a reminder:

> Last call. Four green seals, four berths. Read us the seals from the
> last berth back to the first.

---

## 13. The final plea

**Clip: `the-plea.mp4` — [TO GENERATE, §6]**

The chopper has landed. This is the only round answered in words, not by
choosing:

> The chopper lands. The blast doors give way. The pilot is shouting over
> the rotors: three seats, and I am already over the weight limit. Anyone
> who can state the extraction code walks on now. The rest of you argue it
> out. Give me one good reason why it should be YOU.

Each player gets a private recap of their own night, then two tries at the
keypad if they think they've assembled the code from the four green seals,
then — whether or not that lands — one 150-character argument for why it
should be them.

---

## 14. The tribunal

**Clip: `the-tribunal.mp4` — [TO GENERATE, §7]**

No new narration here — the room reads each other's pleas and votes. This
is the first moment all night a player's own figure sits next to everyone
else's. The clip is the room itself: a ring of people, deciding, lit only
by the chopper's beacon sweeping past.

---

## 15. Result

**Clip: `result.mp4` — [TO GENERATE, §8]**

The night resolves. Who got out by the code, who got out on the numbers,
who the room voted aboard — and, just as often, who didn't, and which of
the three paths nobody claimed at all. The four-digit code from the
manifest is read out here for the first time, spelled out in full:
*"Berth 4 down to berth 1: [digit], [digit], [digit], [digit]."*
